# Toast

Use toast to show information that requires minimal user interaction. Show these event-driven messages by overlaying the message at the bottom left of the screen, as if it was emerging from the navigation sidebar. Toasts can close automatically after 5 seconds, or they may require manual close action.

## Best practices

- Try to keep the text of message concise: it can be up to two lines in length.
- Use dismiss icon to close flags.
- The flag must close after an action is taken, or after 5 seconds. If the flag receives hover status, it should be kept open.
- Use links that allow users to respond to the flag's content.
- Informative icons can be used to make the message clear.

## Usage

Wrap your app once with `ToastProvider` and call `useToast().show()` anywhere inside it. Toasts appear at the bottom left of the screen, close after 5 seconds (`duration`, use `0` to keep them open) and stay open while hovered or focused. They are announced by screen readers through a polite live region.

<Demo src='pages/components/toast/ToastProvider' />

The `type` (`info`, `success`, `warning` or `danger`) sets the informative icon. Use `action` for a link that lets the user respond.

<Demo src='pages/components/toast/ToastTypes' />

### Several toasts

At most three toasts are on the screen at once (`limit` on `ToastProvider`); the next ones wait and show in order as those leave. A toast identical to one on the screen or waiting (same `type`, `title` and text `message`) is not stacked: the one on the screen counts its 5 seconds again, and `show` returns its id.

<Demo src='pages/components/toast/ToastQueue' />

A closed toast fades and slides out, then its space collapses, so the others move into place; with reduced motion it leaves at once. When the toast that closes has the focus, the focus goes to the close button of the next toast, or of the previous one, or back to where it was before it entered the toasts.

## Migrating from ActionableToast

`ActionableToast`, `ActionableToastList` and `useToastMessages` are deprecated and will be removed in a future major version. They map to the new API like this:

| Before                                                | After                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `useToastMessages()` + `<ActionableToastList data />` | `<ToastProvider>` around the app + `useToast()`                              |
| `showToast(options)`                                  | `toast.show(options)`                                                        |
| `secondsVisible` (seconds)                            | `duration` (milliseconds)                                                    |
| `action` + `buttonLabel`                              | `action`: any element, such as a `Link` or a `Button` with its own `onClick` |
| `newToast` (red dot)                                  | no equivalent: use `type` to set the icon and color                          |
| every toast on the screen at once                     | three at most (`limit`), and identical toasts are not stacked                |
