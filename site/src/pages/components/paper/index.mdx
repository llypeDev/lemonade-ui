# Paper

Paper is a surface with a border, rounded corners and an optional shadow, used to group content apart from the page, such as a panel or the box of a form.

## Best practices

- Use the shadow to show what is above the page, such as a floating panel; content that stays in the page usually needs only the border.
- Keep the same elevation for surfaces of the same kind.
- For content with a title and actions, use a `Card`.

## Usage

`Paper` takes any content and the props of a `div`. `elevation` adds a shadow from the theme, from `10` to `240`; without it, the paper has only the border. Add the padding with `style`.

## Accessibility

Paper only changes how the content looks. When it holds a part of the page that users look for, such as a filter panel, add a heading inside it, or a landmark role with a name, such as `role='region'` and `aria-label`.
