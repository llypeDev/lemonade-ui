# Toggletip

The toggletip is a small button, usually beside a label, that shows an explanation when clicked, such as what a field means.

## Best practices

- Use it instead of a `Tooltip` when the explanation has links or buttons, or when it must work on touch screens.
- Keep the explanation short; for longer help, link to a page.
- Do not hide what the user needs to finish a task; show it on the page instead.

## Usage

`Toggletip` takes the explanation as its children. `label` names the button, such as "About billing cycles", `icon` changes its icon, and `placement` chooses the side where the explanation shows.

## Accessibility

The button is named by `label`, "More information" by default, from the locale strings, and tells whether the explanation is open. The explanation is a dialog: the focus moves to its first link or into it, Escape closes it and gives the focus back to the button, and a click outside or moving the focus away also closes it.
