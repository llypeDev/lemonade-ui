# Popover

Popovers are floating panels attached to an element, with interactive content such as filters, short forms or extra details. They open on click and close when the user is done.

## Best practices

- Use popovers for small, related tasks that don't need a page of their own. For a list of actions use [Dropdown](/components/dropdown), and for plain text on hover use [Tooltip](/components/tooltip).
- Keep the content short. If it needs scrolling or many fields, use a [Drawer](/components/drawer) or a [Modal](/components/modal).
- Give the popover a title, so it is clear what it is about.
- Don't open a popover from another popover.

## Usage

Like `Dropdown`, the popover is attached to `anchorRef` and controlled by `open`. It asks to close with `onClose` when `Escape` is pressed, when the focus leaves it and on a click outside it and the anchor. `placement` sets the side (`bottom-start` by default).

## Accessibility

The popover is a dialog: name it with `aria-labelledby` pointing to its title, or with `aria-label`. The anchor receives `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. When it opens, the focus moves to the first focusable element inside it (turn it off with `autoFocus={false}`), and `Escape` gives the focus back to the anchor.
