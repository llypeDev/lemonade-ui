# Meter

The meter shows an amount within a known range, such as storage use, a quota or the seats of a plan.

## Best practices

- Say what is measured in the label, and the value with its unit, such as "7.2 GB of 10 GB".
- Use colors to warn before the limit, with `low`, `high` and `optimum`, and say it in text too when it matters.
- For the progress of a task, use a [Progress circle](/components/progress-circle).

## Usage

`Meter` takes a `value`, a `min` and a `max`, a `label` and a `formatValue` for the value text. With `low`, `high` and `optimum`, as in the HTML `meter` element, the bar is green in the range of the optimum, orange in the next one and red in the farthest; without them, it has the primary color.

## Accessibility

The bar has the `meter` role, named by the label, with the value as text, so screen readers read "Storage, 7.2 GB of 10 GB". Without a visible label, name it with `aria-label`.
